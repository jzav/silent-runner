DEFERRED FOR NOW
----------------------

[deferred] Interactive / exact stdin inheritance behavior
  Reason: needs a fixture that deliberately reads/blocks on inherited stdin.

[deferred] Process-tree termination proof on timeout
  Reason: needs a child fixture that spawns descendants and records survival.

[deferred] Exact Stream-vs-End/replay timing
  Reason: content is covered, but proving arrival while the child is still
          running needs synchronization/timing instrumentation.

[deferred] Exact cross-pipe chronology for mixed stderr / incl-stdout
  Reason: stdout and stderr are drained by separate reader threads; proving
         child write order across both streams needs a synchronized fixture
         or additional instrumentation.
         
[deferred] Buffer-limiter dropped-payload runtime mechanics
  Reason: numeric configuration is covered; deterministic byte/event drop
          accounting needs a controlled high-volume generator fixture.

[deferred] Extreme argv quoting edge cases
  Reason: ordinary spaces/path quoting are covered; exact empty-argument,
          nested-quote, backslash/quote and tab edge cases are better verified
          with a tiny argc/argv fixture executable than a .cmd script.
